You know, my father opened that store in nineteen fifty-one. One window, one bench, one bell on the door. And the first thing he taught me, I was maybe twelve, he hands me a shoe and he says, Sal, bend it. So I bend it. Folds right in half. Like a taco. And he says, now would you walk to church on that? And I said, no. And he said, good. Now you know more than half my customers.
