Seven handbags. That is all I kept from thirty years of buying them, and I am sixty-eight, in Kyoto, five feet tall. [emphatically] Here is the first mistake. A bag that is too big for your frame does not make you look organized. It makes you look like you are moving house. I lived nineteen years in California, in Sunnyvale. I did it the American way too. The giant tote, the strap that slides off the shoulder, the bag so heavy you lean away from it all day. My mother dressed brides in Gion for forty years. She carried a small silk pouch, and nothing else. Tonight I will show you all seven, one by one. [whispering] The last one costs nothing to fix.
